STAYS ON COURSE.

ASCENT is a static browser game with no account or ASCENT back-end. With your permission, limited visitor and gameplay-event data goes directly to Google Analytics so the owner can understand how the game is used.

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 21, 2026

What the game stores

ASCENT uses your browser’s local storage to remember your best score, rotate questions between flights, count completed flights, remember when an advertising break was last shown, and save your analytics choice. This information stays in your browser unless you clear your browser data. It is not used to identify you by ASCENT.

Optional analytics

If you choose Allow analytics, ASCENT loads the Google Tag Manager container GTM-NWWS89ZF. The Google Analytics setup inside that container can count page views, game_start events, and game_complete events.

ASCENT sends no answer text, typed responses, account details, or contact information as analytics events. A completed-game event may include the score, destination reached, and the number of questions completed. Google may still process technical information such as cookie identifiers, IP address, browser, device, and page information under its own terms.

Until you allow analytics, ASCENT does not load Google Tag Manager or Google Analytics. You can change this choice below at any time. Read Google’s Privacy Policy for information about Google’s processing.

Advertising

ASCENT reserves one clearly labelled advertising break after every second completed flight. Google AdSense code is installed while this site is being reviewed. Automatic ads are disabled: a paid display ad will appear only inside that manual flight-break placement after Google approves the site and the owner configures an ad unit.

When a Google AdSense ad unit is active, Google may process information such as IP address, device details, and cookie identifiers to deliver, measure, or personalise ads. The owner must provide the required consent choices before enabling personalised advertising where local law requires it.

For visitors in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, personalised ads should not be enabled without an appropriate consent solution. Learn more in Google’s advertising technologies policy.

Third-party delivery

The game is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process basic technical request data needed to deliver this website, as described in GitHub’s own privacy statement. If you allow analytics, Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics are also third-party services used to collect the limited measurements described above.

Your choices

You can clear ASCENT’s saved game data at any time by clearing this site’s storage in your browser settings. Choosing “Keep analytics off” records that opt-out in ASCENT’s local storage and attempts to remove first-party Google Analytics cookies from this browser. If a third-party advertising partner is enabled later, use that partner’s controls and your browser settings to manage cookies or ad personalisation.

Contact and updates

The site owner should add a support contact before turning on paid advertising. This policy may be updated when the game adds a real ad network, analytics, accounts, or other features that process personal information.
