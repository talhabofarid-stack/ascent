ASCENT · PRIVACY

YOUR DATA
STAYS ON COURSE.

ASCENT is a static browser game. It does not require an account and does not send gameplay information to an ASCENT server.

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 21, 2026

What the game stores

ASCENT uses your browser’s local storage to remember your best score, rotate questions between flights, count completed flights, and remember when an advertising break was last shown. This information stays in your browser unless you clear your browser data. It is not used to identify you.

Advertising

ASCENT has an advertising placement that appears after every third completed flight. At the time of this policy update, no third-party ad network is active in the game unless the site owner configures one.

If the owner enables Google AdSense or another ad partner in the future, that partner may collect information such as IP address, device details, and cookie identifiers to deliver, measure, or personalise ads. The owner must add the required consent choices before enabling personalised advertising where local law requires it.

For visitors in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, personalised ads should not be enabled without an appropriate consent solution. Learn more in Google’s advertising technologies policy.

Third-party delivery

The game is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process basic technical request data needed to deliver this website, as described in GitHub’s own privacy statement. ASCENT does not run its own analytics service in this version of the game.

Your choices

You can clear ASCENT’s saved game data at any time by clearing this site’s storage in your browser settings. If a third-party advertising partner is enabled later, use that partner’s controls and your browser settings to manage cookies or ad personalisation.

Contact and updates

The site owner should add a support contact before turning on paid advertising. This policy may be updated when the game adds a real ad network, analytics, accounts, or other features that process personal information.

This page is a plain-language privacy notice for the current static version of ASCENT. It is not legal advice.
